# Independent Review Checklist
1. Did the Stage Goal actually complete?
2. What is Blocking vs Non-blocking?
3. Is runtime evidence real and bound to the reviewed baseline?
4. Any false-success, evidence rewriting, bypass, fail-open, dirty-overlap, or unreviewed destructive path?
5. Any overdesign, duplicate governance, premature abstraction?
6. Is Sign-Off evidence sufficient?
7. What is the minimum complete next Stage?
