# Work Class Policy
- `SMALL_CHANGE`: local low-risk change; direct change + necessary check.
- `STAGE_WORK`: system/multi-file/semantic/runtime Stage; requires preflight, contract impact when applicable, Unit/Path/Runtime evidence and independent review.
- `FORMAL_ACCEPTANCE`: release/holdout/production acceptance; additionally requires frozen criteria, binding and stronger review/human gates.
Global safety applies to all classes.
